## About me

I like bringing everyday life and technology together: trying new tools, tinkering with small ideas, and making things just because they seem interesting. Some turn out to be useful; others are simply fun to mess around with.

Alongside my studies, I have experience in Japanese rental operations, where I use AI and IT tools to help organize information and everyday tasks.

## Research & education

**2026–2028 (expected) · Master's, Ritsumeikan University**<br>
Graduate School of Information Science and Engineering · [Digital Governance Systems Laboratory](https://dgovsys.org/members/sun-wenhuan/)

My current research concerns petition success on Taiwan's JOIN platform. I am exploring how petition text, patterns of public support over time, and wider social attention relate to petition outcomes. This research is ongoing.

**2022–2026 · Undergraduate, Ritsumeikan University**<br>
College of Information Science and Engineering · Information Systems Science and Engineering (ISSE)

I studied robotics, control, image processing, and human–computer interaction. My graduation research explored single-hand text input: MediaPipe hand landmarks and optional RealSense depth cues feed a T9-style input system, with dwell-time confirmation to reduce accidental selections. [Code and project materials →](https://github.com/sunfhs/RGBD-T9-Gesture-Input)
